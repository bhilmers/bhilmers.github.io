Mission

Studio Sagan turns imagination into reality. Whether your idea is fuzzy or crystal clear, Studio Sagan can refine and realize your concepts into digital and physical space. It can be something simple like branding and social media assets or something complex like designing and prototyping a product idea. Everything starts with a conversation, thoughts to words to actions, and eventually goals.

3D Printing

We print PLA in-house with a volume up to 180mm3 and use a trusted vendor for other materials and larger sizes. If you are looking for a custom or replacement part for something you own, please understand these two things:

First, it is difficult to design and print parts without the original in hand, but not impossible. We require at minimum a good attempt at drawing the desired part with dimensions in millimeters. If you own a pair of digital calipers this will make things easier.

Second, prototyping takes iterations. It may take two or three tries before we get an exact fit, though occasionally the first try is a success.
